Greeting Arm Simulator WAVE-1

This is a simulator. It does not control physical hardware.
Use MotionControl.home before MotionControl.wave. The wave command accepts
1 to 3 cycles and an amplitude from 5 to 30 degrees. The simulated arm returns
to zero after a wave. For real arms, verify the installation, clear the
workspace, and follow the manufacturer's safety procedures and rated limits.
